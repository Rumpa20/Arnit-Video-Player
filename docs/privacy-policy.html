Privacy Policy

Arnit Video Player · Last updated: 6 November 2026

Arnit Video Player is an Android application developed by Arnit Creation. This policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information and local media

The app lets you browse and play video files stored on your device. Android may ask you to grant media or file access so the app can display and play the videos you choose. Your local video and audio files are processed on your device. The app does not upload your personal media files to a server operated by the developer.

Online playback and network access

If you choose to play an online video, your device connects to the URL or service you provide or select. The relevant website or service may receive technical information such as your IP address and request details under its own privacy policy. The developer does not control those third-party services.

Chromecast and third-party services

If you use casting features, the app may communicate with compatible Cast devices and Google Cast services. Those services are provided by third parties and are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Accounts, advertising and payments

Arnit Video Player does not require you to create an account. The app does not include in-app purchases, paid subscriptions or advertising.

Data collection and sharing

The developer does not operate an account or media-upload service for this app. The app's playback features use information on your device and the network destinations you choose. We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by Android, Google Cast, or an online video provider when you use their platform or services, subject to their own policies.

Data security and retention

Your local media remains on your device unless you choose to share it using Android or another app. The developer does not maintain a server-side account or media library for you. You can remove the app's local data or uninstall the app through Android settings.

Children's privacy

Arnit Video Player is a general-purpose media player and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, please contact us.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown above.
